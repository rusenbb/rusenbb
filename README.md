# Hey, I'm Ruşen

I'm an AI researcher and engineer based in Ankara, Turkey, and an AI & Data Engineering graduate from Istanbul Technical University. My research interests center on the robustness and reliability of large language models.

I work as an independent researcher with [METU’s Applied NLP Group](https://nlp.ceng.metu.edu.tr), collaborating with Çağrı Toraman on Turkic-language evaluation and Turkish LLM development. I also lead [METU NLP Connect](https://www.youtube.com/@metunlpconnect), producing expert interviews. Previously, I co-founded Fiction Studios with a friend and served as its technical lead.

## What I work with

- **Research and engineering:** NLP, LLMs, model evaluation, retrieval-augmented generation, and machine unlearning
- **Tools:** PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, PEFT/LoRA, Unsloth, and ROS2
- **Languages:** Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, SQL, and C/C++

## Selected projects

- **[AIZheimer](https://github.com/rusenbb/ai-zheimer):** Experiments with cross-attention-based machine unlearning in Stable Diffusion 2.1, building on Forget-Me-Not.
- **[Biting The Bytes](https://github.com/rusen-archive/Biting-The-Bytes):** Turkish diacritic restoration with ByT5-small and LoRA. I took primary responsibility for training and hyperparameter tuning, with debugging and deployment shared with a teammate.
- **[To-AI-or-Not-to-AI](https://github.com/rusen-archive/To-AI-or-Not-to-AI):** An ensemble of three fine-tuned RoBERTa detectors for AI-generated text, deployed on Hugging Face Spaces.
- **[Tobor](https://github.com/rusen-archive/Amazon-Warehouse-Robot):** A team-built autonomous warehouse robot using ROS2 and YOLO. I implemented its box pickup and drop-off mechanism, shared annotation work, and supported other algorithms.
- **Kaira:** My undergraduate capstone exploring financial reasoning in Qwen-based LLMs through GRPO fine-tuning with Unsloth and LoRA.
